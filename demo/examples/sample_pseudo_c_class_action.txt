Class action claim filed on 4 March 2025 against MegaCorp International. Lead plaintiffs: Robert Anderson, Yuki Tanaka, Priya Sharma, Carlos Mendez, and Nina Petrov. The action concerns unpaid overtime for warehouse staff at MegaCorp's distribution centre in Birmingham between 2021 and 2024. Anderson, Tanaka, Sharma, Mendez, and Petrov each seek damages of approximately £35,000. MegaCorp is represented by Bishop & Wright LLP.
