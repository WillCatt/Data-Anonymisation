CLINICAL NEGLIGENCE — LETTER OF CLAIM

To: The Chief Executive, Westmoor Hospitals NHS Trust, Ardleigh Road, Norwich NR3 2PL.

Dear Sirs,

We act for Mr Tomasz Wiśniewski, date of birth 4 July 1961, of 17 Bellhaven Close, Wymondham NR18 0QS, NHS number 486 227 3910.

BACKGROUND

Mr Wiśniewski, a Polish national resident in the United Kingdom since 1998, worked as a machine operator until 2021. He has type 2 diabetes, diagnosed in 2014, and a documented allergy to penicillin recorded on his file since 2009.

On 2 February 2022 our client attended the Emergency Department at Westmoor General Hospital reporting abdominal pain and a temperature of 38.9°C. He was seen by Dr Rebecca Ainsworth, ST4, at approximately 21:40. Bloods showed a white cell count of 19.2 and CRP of 214. Dr Ainsworth recorded a working diagnosis of gastroenteritis and discharged him at 01:15 on 3 February 2022.

Our client re-attended on 5 February 2022 in septic shock. He underwent emergency laparotomy that evening under the care of Mr Idris Farouk, Consultant General Surgeon, who found a perforated appendix with generalised peritonitis. Mr Wiśniewski spent 11 days in the Critical Care Unit and a further 26 days on Ward 14.

ALLEGATIONS OF BREACH

It is alleged that the Trust, by its servants or agents, failed to act on inflammatory markers plainly indicating intra-abdominal sepsis; failed to arrange CT imaging on 2 February 2022; and discharged our client contrary to the Trust's own Sepsis Screening Protocol (version 4.1, issued March 2021). Dr Ainsworth's entry does not record a NEWS2 score, which the protocol requires.

We have obtained a preliminary report from Professor Alison Wrexham, Consultant in Emergency Medicine, dated 28 August 2023, and expect a causation report from Mr Samuel Oduya-Grant in due course.

CAUSATION AND DAMAGES

But for the delay, our client would have undergone appendicectomy on 2 February 2022 and avoided peritonitis, a stoma reversed in November 2022, and an abdominal hernia requiring repair. He has not returned to work. Past loss of earnings to date is £58,300; future loss is pleaded on a multiplier of 4.6.

We invite the Trust's Letter of Response within four months, by 9 February 2024, in accordance with the Pre-Action Protocol for the Resolution of Clinical Disputes.
