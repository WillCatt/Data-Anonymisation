Maria Petrova has filed a claim against Acme Holdings Ltd in the dispute concerning the contract dated 12 March 2018. Mrs Petrova, a Bulgarian national, alleges that John Smith, the company's chief financial officer, breached his fiduciary duty. Acme denies all wrongdoing and is represented by Sofia District Court. Smith has not yet responded to the allegations, while Petrova continues to seek damages of approximately €450,000.
