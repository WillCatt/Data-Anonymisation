EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNAL — CASE MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

Claim no. 2402447/2024, lodged 14 March 2024. Claimant: Dr Amara Okonjo-Bright, of 42 Marchmont Row, Leeds LS6 2AT. Respondent: Northgate Logistics Group plc, registered office 8 Calder Wharf, Leeds LS1 4DP, company number 04982117.

THE PARTIES

Dr Okonjo-Bright, a Nigerian-born British citizen aged 41, was employed as Head of Network Planning by Northgate from 3 September 2018 until her dismissal on 22 January 2024. Her final annual salary was £96,400 plus a discretionary bonus of up to 18%. She holds a doctorate in operational research from the University of Sheffield and is a practising Muslim. Okonjo-Bright reported to Mr Callum Beattie, Operations Director, throughout the relevant period.

Northgate operates a distribution network of 240 vehicles from depots in Leeds, Warrington and Motherwell. The company employs approximately 1,850 staff. Beattie has been with Northgate since 2011.

THE COMPLAINT

The claimant alleges direct discrimination on grounds of race and religion, contrary to sections 13 and 39 of the Equality Act 2010, together with unfair dismissal under section 98 of the Employment Rights Act 1996.

She says that from around November 2022 she was excluded from the Fleet Decarbonisation Programme ("FDP"), a project she had designed, and that Mr Beattie appointed Mr Stephen Halloway to lead the FDP on 6 February 2023 despite Halloway having no relevant experience. On 19 April 2023 she raised a formal grievance with Human Resources. The grievance was investigated by Ms Priya Raghavan of the People and Culture team and dismissed on 30 June 2023.

The claimant further alleges that during a meeting on 11 December 2023 Beattie remarked that clients "expect a certain kind of face on the account". Ms Raghavan was present. The respondent denies the remark was made.

Following a restructure announced on 8 January 2024, the claimant's role was placed at risk. She was dismissed on 22 January 2024 with 12 weeks' pay in lieu of notice, totalling £22,246. Two comparator roles were retained, held by Halloway and by Mr Daniel Roche.

REMEDY SOUGHT

Compensation of £185,000, comprising loss of earnings to 31 December 2025, injury to feelings in the upper Vento band, and interest at 8%. The claimant is now employed part-time by Riverside Freight Solutions Ltd on £41,000.
